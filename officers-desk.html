Officer's Desk

Javed Iqbal Gulshan
Chief Education Officer, Poonch (J&K)

Javed Iqbal Gulshan is a distinguished academic leader and senior administrator who serves as the Chief Education Officer of Poonch district, Jammu & Kashmir. Known for a long, impactful career within the School Education Department—including a standout tenure as Principal of Government Higher Secondary School, Harni (Mendhar)—Mr. Gulshan is widely recognized for his visionary, ground-level leadership that prioritizes accountability, surprise institutional audits, infrastructure upgrades, and rural literacy.

Message to the Community: As a collaborative administrator, his core message to the general public emphasizes that education is a collective social responsibility, urging parents and community stakeholders to actively partner with public schools to build a supportive ecosystem for every child.

Message to Students: Simultaneously, his inspiring message to students encourages them to look beyond their immediate circumstances, advising them to embrace discipline, curiosity, and hard work as their ultimate tools to unlock a bright and limitless future.
